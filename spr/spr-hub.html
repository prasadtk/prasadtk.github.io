Reusable Chip Regeneration & Re-Capture Cycles

Biotin CAPture Chip (ssDNA Hybridization)Overcomes irreversible Streptavidin (SA) binding. Hybridize Biotin-CAPture reagent (ssDNA-SA), capture biotinylated ligand, run analyte, and strip completely with 6 M Guanidine-HCl + 0.25 M NaOH for 100+ fresh ligand cycles.
NTA & Tris-NTA Chips (Ni²⁺ / Co²⁺ Chelation)Captures 6x–10xHis-tagged proteins gently at neutral pH. Strip old/denatured ligand with 350 mM EDTA (pH 8.3), wash, and recharge with 0.5 mM NiCl₂ (or CoCl₂ for higher stability) before capturing fresh ligand.
Anti-His / Anti-GST / Anti-Human Fc CaptureCovalently immobilize high-affinity capture antibodies (e.g., anti-His mAb or anti-human IgG Fc) on CM5/CM4. Capture fresh tagged ligand every cycle and regenerate the capture antibody with 10 mM Glycine-HCl pH 1.5–2.0.

Immobilization Coupling Chemistries Reference

Amine Coupling (EDC / NHS)Targets primary amines (-NH₂ on Lysine/N-terminus). Activate with 0.4 M EDC + 0.1 M NHS (7 min), inject ligand in 10 mM Sodium Acetate at pH 4.0–5.5 (0.5–1.0 units below ligand pI), block with 1 M Ethanolamine-HCl pH 8.5.
Thiol / Ligand-Thiol Coupling (PDEA)Targets free cysteines (-SH) or introduces reactive disulfides via PDEA. Ideal for site-directed orientation of engineered Cys-mutants; block with 50 mM L-Cysteine + 1 M NaCl pH 4.3.
Aldehyde / Hydrazide CouplingOxidize cis-diols on glycoproteins with 10 mM NaIO₄, activate surface with 5 mM Carbohydrazide, couple at pH 4.0–4.5, and stabilize hydrazone bonds with 0.1 M NaCNBH₃.
Click Chemistry (DBCO–Azide / Maleimide)Copper-free strain-promoted azide-alkyne cycloaddition (SPAAC) at neutral pH 6.5–7.5 for ultra-gentle, homogeneous site-specific immobilization.

Systematic Regeneration Scouting Ladder

Step 1: Start MildInject short (15–30 s) pulses at 30 µL/min of 10 mM Glycine-HCl pH 3.0 or 1 M NaCl. Check for <5 RU baseline drift and >90% binding retention.
Step 2: Escalate or CombineStep down pH (2.5 → 2.0 → 1.5) or switch to multi-component cocktails (ionic + chaotropic + mild detergent) rather than harsh single acids.
Step 3: Single-Cycle Kinetics (SCK)If no regeneration preserves ligand activity, inject 5 ascending analyte concentrations sequentially without regeneration and fit with an SCK model.

Pre-Run & Immobilization Checklist

Filter (0.22 µm) and degas all running buffers thoroughly to prevent air spikes.
Perform pH scouting (pH 4.0–5.5 in 10 mM Sodium Acetate) below ligand pI for amine coupling.
Leave Flow Cell 1 (Fc1) blank-activated/blocked as an in-line reference surface.
Match DMSO and surfactant (P20) concentrations exactly between analyte dilutions and running buffer.
Include at least 2–3 buffer blank cycles for double-referencing subtraction.
Use high flow rates (30–50 µL/min) during kinetic runs to minimize mass transport limitation.

Buffer Selection & Additive Guidelines

Detergent / Surfactant (0.005%–0.05% P20 or Tween-20)Prevents protein adsorption to microfluidic tubing and reduces non-specific hydrophobic sticking. Omit strictly on L1/HPA lipid chips.
Reducing Agents (TCEP vs. DTT)Prefer 0.5–1 mM TCEP over DTT for maintaining free cysteines, as DTT rapidly reduces Ni²⁺ on NTA chips and degrades gold-thiol monolayers.
Soluble Dextran / BSA Carrier (NSB Suppression)Add 1 mg/mL carboxymethyl-dextran (CMD) and/or 0.1–0.5 mg/mL BSA to running buffer when screening sticky analytes or serum samples.
