Universal Method Setup Guidelines
Core rules of thumb for high-quality kinetic and affinity sensorgrams

1. Analyte Concentration Range

Inject 5 to 8 serial dilutions spanning 0.1× KD to 10× KD. Include buffer blanks for double-referencing and at least one duplicate middle concentration.

2. Contact (Association) Time

Use 60–180 seconds for standard proteins. Fast-on/fast-off small molecules only need 30–60 s; slow-associating complexes may require 180–300 s.

3. Dissociation Time

Use 120–600 seconds. For tight antibodies (KD < 1 nM), extend dissociation to 900–1,800 s to observe at least a 5% signal decay for accurate kd fitting.

4. Regeneration & Buffer Match

Start mild (10 mM Glycine-HCl pH 2.0–2.5 or 1–2 M NaCl for 15–30 s). Match running buffer DMSO and detergent percentages exactly across all samples.
