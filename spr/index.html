4. Universal SPR Assay Setup Guidelines

Concentration Series (0.1× – 10× KD)

Inject 5 to 8 serial dilutions spanning two orders of magnitude around your expected KD to capture both initial binding curvature and saturation.

Association (Contact) Time

Use 60–180 s for standard proteins. Fast-on/fast-off small molecules need only 30–60 s, whereas slow binders may require 180–300 s.

Dissociation Time

Use 120–600 s for routine kinetics. For tight antibodies (KD < 1 nM), extend to 900–1,800 s to observe at least a 5% signal decay.

Double Referencing & Regeneration

Subtract both a reference flow cell and blank buffer injections. Start regeneration mild (10 mM Glycine-HCl pH 2.0–2.5 or 1–2 M NaCl for 15–30 s).
